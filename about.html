Our story

What are we?

Dakota STEM Trails, housed in Brookings, South Dakota, is a student-led organization working to make STEM education more accessible to children in rural communities.

Through hands-on activities, creative challenges, workshops, and community partnerships, we bring meaningful STEM experiences directly to students and help them discover their potential as future scientists, engineers, innovators, and problem-solvers.

What we do

How we bring STEM to students

Hands-on activities

Kids learn by asking questions, testing ideas, and building models.

Creative challenges

Problems to solve that turn curiosity into real STEM skills.

Workshops

Programs that travel to schools, libraries, and community centers.

Community partnerships

Partners who share mentorship, expertise, venues, and resources.

Our mission

To inspire and encourage children across rural South Dakota to explore science, technology, engineering, and mathematics through accessible, creative, and community-centered learning opportunities.
